# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints,
and accepting constructive criticism. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized
language or imagery, trolling, and personal or political attacks.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainer via a private advisory or the project issues, and
will be reviewed and responded to within 5 business days.
